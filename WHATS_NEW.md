---
title: Tags and combined Tag Reports
date: 2026-11-20
---
You can now **tag your audits** to group them by department, site, project, or whatever works for your team. Add tags in the **Audit Builder**, or at any time from the top of an audit's page. Tags are shared across your organization, and **Filter by Tags** on the Audits screen shows only the audits you choose. [Learn more about organizing audits with tags](https://equalify.uic.edu/dashboard/user-guide/2-configuring-and-running-audits/2-5-organizing-audits-with-tags).

Select any tag to open its **Tag Report**: one view that combines every audit with that tag. Track their total blockers over time on a single chart, see which audits and pages need the most attention, and add or remove audits right from the report. [Learn more about Tag Reports](https://equalify.uic.edu/dashboard/user-guide/3-interpreting-audit-data-and-dashboards/3-7-tag-reports).

Admins can rename, merge, or delete tags under **Account > Tags** to keep the list tidy.
