# Notifications and Sharing

## Email Notifications
You can set up email notifications to keep yourself or your team updated on audit results.
- Notifications can be added when creating or editing an audit.
- Frequency options include:
    - Weekly
    - Daily
    - Monthly
- This ensures you don't have to manually check the dashboard to stay informed.

## Sharing Reports
You can share audit reports with others, even if they don't have an Equalify account.
1.  Click the **Share** button on an audit report.
2.  This generates a **publicly accessible URL**.
3.  You can copy and send this URL to your team or stakeholders.

**Note**:
- Sharing a report allows others to **view** it.
- It does **not** give them permission to edit the report. Only users with administrative credentials and proper access can edit audits.
