This page is intended for **all users** who encounter bugs or require support. Any accessibility issue with the platform is considered a mission-critical bug. We appreciate the community contributing to our bug reports and sharing any issues that they find. When problems arise, we tackle them head-on, and the progress of fixing them is tracked publicly on GitHub.

## How to report bugs:
1. Go to the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/equalifyEverything/equalify/issues).
1. Click New Issue.
1. Choose Bug or Feature Request.
1. Fill in the template (describe the bug, steps to reproduce, etc.).
1. The engineering team monitors these issues.
